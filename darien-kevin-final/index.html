About Us

NutriTrack's mission is to make nutrition information simple, accessible, and easy to understand. Our website pulls information from different databases and sources, allowing users to search for foods and find nutritional information that best matches what they are eating.

Whether you're tracking your diet, doing personal research, or simply curious about nutrition, we want NutriTrack to be useful for everyone.

API Credit

NutriTrack uses the USDA FoodData Central API to provide nutrition and food information.
